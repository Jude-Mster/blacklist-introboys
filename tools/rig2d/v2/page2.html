What changed

  • A proper base body: head, torso, both legs, and both arms as separate jointed pieces (shoulder, elbow, wrist, hand). Joints bend smoothly with no seams.
  • Real hands: the spear hand is your own fist from the recording, closed on the shaft. When both hands are needed, the other hand closes into a fist and slides onto the shaft too.
  • How it holds the weapon: the spear has a real direction in depth. It shortens as it points toward or away from you, and passes behind the body or in front of it.
  • Skyfall: crouch and coil, lift the spear overhead, sweep it in a full circle around the body with a green-gold trail, red burst, recover.

Ready for costumes

  • The skeleton belongs to the base body. A costume is a picture in the same idle pose with its joints marked. It's cut into the same pieces (body, arms, hands, extras like wings) and fitted onto the bones.
  • Your current armour is the first costume, wings included. Switch it off to see the base body underneath.
  • New armour or a custom mesh later goes through the same path, so every move works with it straight away.
Still a preview: the body is flat (2D), so it can't truly turn its back. In the sweep the torso twists and leans while the spear does the turning in depth. It's not in the arena yet.
