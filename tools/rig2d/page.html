Building the rig…

Piece by piece

  • Cut from your frame: the recording's spear, its aura, the mouse cursor and the halo remnants are removed. The big wing is cut onto its own layer behind the body.
  • 18 bones: hips, waist, neck and head, both arms, both legs (thigh, shin, foot), the cape, and both wings in two parts.
  • One bending mesh of about 4,000 points over the body, each tied to up to four bones, so joints bend smoothly.
  • Feet stay planted: the legs solve their knees to keep the feet on the floor while the hips move, crouch and lunge.
  • Follow-through: the head, wings, cape and the hanging ring lag and settle on springs, so nothing stops dead.
  • Held in both hands: each arm is two armoured segments (the crimson guard from your frame) with a gauntleted fist. The front hand holds the spear where the move puts it, the back hand grips the shaft behind it, and both elbows bend to reach.
Where this stands: it's a prototype built from one game frame. Parts hidden in that frame (the far arm, the back of the cape) can't move far without showing gaps, so big arm swings and full spins stay with the recorded flipbook. It's not in the arena yet. If you like the direction, the next step is wiring it into the arena for the moves the recording doesn't have (the clash bind, the hit recoil, stepping in), and keeping the recording for the Skyfall spin.
